> Simple physics question. No tools needed. Three short sentences.

Air around us always contains some water vapor, and the cold glass chills the thin layer of air touching its surface. Cool air can hold less vapor, so the excess water condenses into liquid on the glass. Those droplets come from the surrounding air — not from water leaking through the glass.
