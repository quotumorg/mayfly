Air always holds some invisible water vapor, and the amount it can hold drops as temperature falls. The glass chills the air touching its surface below the point where that vapor can stay gaseous. So the vapor condenses into liquid droplets on the outside of the glass — the water comes from the air, not from leaking through the glass.
