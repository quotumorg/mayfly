> Simple physics question. No tools needed. Three short sentences, plain text.

The cold glass chills the thin layer of air touching its surface. Air always holds some invisible water vapor, and cooler air can hold less of it. That excess vapor condenses into liquid droplets on the outside of the glass — the water comes from the air, not through the glass.
